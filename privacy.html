Privacy Policy

FLIP: The Dealer · Effective June 26, 2026
In short: FLIP does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Your game progress stays on your device.

Information We Collect

None. FLIP: The Dealer does not collect any personal data. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not request your name, email, contacts, location, photos, or any other personal information.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your game progress (cash, inventory, reputation, collection, and settings) is saved locally on your device only, using your device's local storage. This data never leaves your phone and is not transmitted to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes this data.

Network Use

The game makes a single type of anonymous network request: it fetches live gold and Bitcoin reference prices from a public market data provider (CoinGecko) to display in-game market indices. This request contains no personal information about you and is used only to show live prices. If the request fails, the game continues to work normally.

Notifications

With your permission, FLIP may send local notifications (for example, "a customer is at your desk"). These are generated on your device and are not push notifications from a server. You can disable them at any time in your device Settings.

Advertising & Analytics

FLIP contains no third-party advertising and no analytics or tracking SDKs.

Children's Privacy

FLIP does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app contains no chat, no user-generated content sharing, and no data collection.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
