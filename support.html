How to Play

You run a watch & diamond dealer's desk. Customers walk in to sell or buy. Roll the dice to set the tone, then haggle across five rounds — push too hard and they walk. Buy low, sell high, examine pieces to spot fakes, build your reputation, and climb from street hustler to living legend. Hunt rare grails and hold them to grow your fame.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your device, locally. Deleting the app erases your save. There are no accounts or cloud sync.

Customers arrive slowly. Can I speed it up?

Yes — tap Promote on Socials at the desk to bring customers in quickly for the next hour.

Why does the game ask for notifications?

So it can tell you when a customer arrives while the app is closed. This is optional and can be turned off in your device Settings.

What are grails?

Legendary, rare pieces that appear as your fame grows. Owning one boosts your reputation — but selling one drops it. True collectors hold.

Does the game collect my data?

No. FLIP collects no personal data. See our Privacy Policy.

Is there an internet connection required?

No. The game works fully offline; an optional connection only fetches live gold/Bitcoin prices for the in-game market.
